Underground Layout Editor

Tunnel: drag from square to square to draw a tunnel. Corners and junctions shape themselves. Dragging into a hideaway gives it a door on that side.

Small hideaway: click a square to turn it into a small hideaway, or back into a tunnel.

Big hideaway: click to place one. It covers 2 by 2 squares.

Erase: click or drag to turn squares back into rock. Right-click also erases with any tool.

The top two rows and the left column hold the secret base rooms.

Each square is one map block of 32 by 32 tiles. Tunnels are 5 tiles wide.
