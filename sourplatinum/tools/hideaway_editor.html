Rock height
Water depth

Rock rises one storey per tile, like the cave walls. Height 2 is the normal cave wall. Water at depth 0 is a pond or river: it sits just below the ground with an overworld-style shoreline. Depth 1 or 2 makes a deeper pit with cave-wall banks (good for lava).

Textures 6

Paints floors, water and the flat tops of rock. None leaves a rock top black, like the cave walls. Bright grass, path, sand, snow, puddles and lakes get rounded edges and corners automatically on the floor around them, using the same edge tiles as the overworld.

Objects 7

Objects stand on the level of the tile under their corner and block the tiles they cover (flowers, craters and pools stay walkable). R turns the next object.

Adding it to the game

Drag to paint. Hold Shift while dragging to fill a rectangle. Right-click a tile to pick up its terrain, height and texture.

Exit goes in a straight cave wall (height 2) right next to the floor, with rock on both sides. It becomes the doorway back to the Underground. Use one exit.

Save the file into tools/underground_block_editor_assets/hideaways, then run py -3 install_hideaways.py in the tools folder.
